The Warrior is EmberQuest's frontline tank: the class with a real armor rating and a crowd-control kit. If you want to be the person the boss is looking at while everyone else does the work, this is your class.

## What does the Warrior do well?

Two things, and only two things matter: **survivability and control**. The Warrior has the highest base HP (14.1) and the only meaningful base armor rating (0.8) among the four classes, plus a 6.1% resist chance against impairing effects like slow, poison, hook, and push. In a game where knockbacks and hooks are a real threat — including in PvP — that resist profile is quietly one of the strongest stats on the class.

The control side is why groups want one: crowd control is the Warrior's stated specialty, which in practice means managing pulls and protecting squishier players rather than topping damage charts.

## What are the Warrior's base stats?

From the official wiki's live display (checked **October 11, 2026**, before gear; values change with balance patches):

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| HP | 14.1 |
| Armor | 0.8 |
| Resist (slow/poison/hook/push) | 6.1% |
| Heal bonus | 8.2% |
| Pierce | 5.7% |
| Crit chance | 7.0% |
| Crit damage | 54.1% |
| Backstab | 4.1% |
| Combat speed | 8.2% |
| Dash profile | 1.65× speed, 1× stamina |

The funny detail: the Warrior's **crit damage (54.1%) is the second-highest in the game** — higher than the Archer and Ranger. Base crit chance is still the modest part; think of the Warrior as someone who blocks, controls, and then occasionally hits very hard, not a raw DPS pick.

## How mobile is the Warrior?

The dash profile is the standard 1.65× speed at 1× stamina cost — no mobility bonus, no penalty. You move like a normal character and rely on armor rather than footwork. The 8.2% combat speed bonus is the highest of the four classes and quietly helps in fights where repositioning one step decides whether a swing lands.

## When should you pick Warrior?

- You play in a group and want the anchor role.
- You want the class that survives your own learning curve — 14.1 HP forgives the most mistakes.
- You intend to fight in PvP zones, where the armor and resist profile shine.
- You like the "hold the line while the plan happens" fantasy.

Avoid it if you exclusively solo and want kills at range: the Warrior's damage comes from being in the scary place, and the [Archer](/classes/archer/) or [Assassin](/classes/assassin/) will clear solo content faster with escape tools the Warrior simply does not have.

## What should a Warrior train first?

Weapon choice is open (see the [crafting overview](/crafting/crafting-overview/) for what tier 1–3 gear costs), but pairing the tank's durability with a melee skill keeps you in your natural range. Frontline picks: **Melee** as your combat skill, **Mining and Chopping** as your money skills since both feed metal-and-wood armor crafting — the exact armor type the Warrior wants to wear anyway. Full skill breakdown: [skills overview](/skills/skills-overview/).

## How does the Warrior compare to the others?

Against the [Ranger](/classes/ranger/) (the other durable pick), the Warrior trades flexibility for a bigger armor stat and control kit. Against the [Archer](/classes/archer/) and [Assassin](/classes/assassin/): completely different games — they avoid damage, you stand in it.
