The Ranger is EmberQuest's balanced skirmisher: the class that gives up the [Warrior's](/classes/warrior/) armor wall and the [Assassin's](/classes/assassin/) burst without becoming fragile in the process. If "I want to be okay at everything" sounds like a cop-out, the stat sheet disagrees — the Ranger quietly owns the best movement economy in the game.

## What are the Ranger's base stats?

From the official wiki's live display (checked **October 11, 2026**, before gear; values will move with patches):

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| HP | 13.1 |
| Armor | 0.5 |
| Resist | 6.1% |
| Heal bonus | 8.2% |
| Pierce | 0% |
| Crit chance | 6.0% |
| Crit damage | 50.0% |
| Backstab | 8.2% |
| Combat speed | 4.1% |
| Dash profile | 1.65× speed, 1× stamina |

The highlights: **second-highest HP (13.1)**, a middle-class armor value (0.5) that actually reduces incoming damage, the same 6.1% resist line as the Warrior, and — the sleeper stat — **the standard dash speed at only 1× stamina cost**, the best dash economy in the game. The Archer dashes faster but pays 3.54× stamina; the Assassin pays 4.57×. The Ranger just keeps dashing, forever.

## What is the Ranger actually good at?

Ranged utility with sustain. The kit is described as balanced with "sustain and ranged utility" — meaning you have ranged tools, some self-maintenance, and no glaring hole. The 8.2% backstab is the second-highest in the game (double the Warrior's and Archer's), which makes the Ranger surprisingly competent at opportunistic PvP: dash-in economics work because dashes are cheap for you.

Solo, this adds up to the most forgiving ranged class: enough HP and armor to survive a mistake, enough mobility to correct it, and heal bonus that makes food and potions stretch further.

## How does the combat speed bonus feel?

4.1% combat speed is modest on paper — it is not a sprint mode. In practice, "move a little faster while fighting" compounds with the cheap dash to make the Ranger the best class at fighting while retreating, which is 90% of solo survivability in an action MMO.

## When should you pick Ranger?

- You play solo most of the time and want the lowest-risk class.
- You want ranged play without 11 HP ([Archer](/classes/archer/)) or 10.5 HP ([Assassin](/classes/assassin/)).
- You want to dabble in PvP without building a dedicated PvP character — the cheap dashes and backstab let you play opportunistically.
- You value a class that no balance patch is likely to gut: balanced stats rarely get hit hardest.

## When should you not pick Ranger?

If you want a specialization with a ceiling: the [Warrior](/classes/warrior/) tanks harder, the [Archer](/classes/archer/) pierces harder, the [Assassin](/classes/assassin/) bursts harder. The Ranger is the pick for people who want none of those ceilings to matter.

## What should a Ranger train first?

Combat: **Archery** is the natural home for a ranged skirmisher. Money skills: **Fishing and Spearfishing** lean into the ranged, coast-side lifestyle and feed consumable crafting; **Collecting** is a low-intensity alternative that pairs with exploration.
