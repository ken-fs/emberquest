The Assassin is EmberQuest's burst-damage class and the answer to a question that comes up constantly: **who can stealth?** Officially: only Assassins, and only through consumable boosts — every other class is locked out entirely. That exclusivity, plus the game's highest crit stats and a massive backstab bonus, defines the class.

## What are the Assassin's base stats?

From the official wiki's live display (checked **October 11, 2026**, before gear — expect tuning at the Steam launch):

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| HP | 10.5 |
| Armor | 0.1 |
| Resist | 2.0% |
| Heal bonus | 4.1% |
| Pierce | 0% |
| Crit chance | 13.2% |
| Crit damage | 62.2% |
| Backstab | 32.7% |
| Dash profile | 2.24× speed, 4.57× stamina |

Read the extremes: **the lowest HP, the lowest resist, half the healing bonus of other classes — and the game's highest crit chance (13.2%), highest crit damage (62.2%), an enormous 32.7% backstab bonus, and the fastest dash (2.24×) at the steepest stamina cost (4.57×).** The Assassin is the class that either kills or dies, and the game built it that way on purpose.

## How does stealth actually work?

Stealth is a **consumable boost** — you get access to it because you are an Assassin, but it is something you activate with a consumable rather than a toggled ability, which means stealth has a resource economy: every sneaky approach costs you an item. In PvP that economy defines the Assassin's threat model — being stealthed is a limited resource, not a permanent state, so good players bait it out.

## What is the backstab bonus worth?

The 32.7% backstab bonus is 4–8× what any other class gets (Warrior 4.1%, Archer 4.1%, Ranger 8.2%). Combined with the crit profile, an opening hit from behind is by far the highest-damage single action any class can land in EmberQuest. If you enjoy position-based combat — using the terrain, timing approached, and choosing your moment — this is the most technical class in the game.

## When should you pick Assassin?

- You want the game's highest skill ceiling and its most distinctive perk (stealth).
- You plan to play PvP (world zones, arenas) where positioning beats stats.
- You like consumable-driven gameplay — poisons and boosts are part of your rotation, not an afterthought.
- You can live with 10.5 HP. Seriously: you will die during the learning process, and that is the class working as intended.

## When should you not pick Assassin?

If you want to solo the cozy parts of the game in comfort, or if dying repeatedly during boss learning sounds miserable: the [Ranger](/classes/ranger/) gives up a bit of speed for 13.1 HP and real armor, and the [Warrior](/classes/warrior/) straight-up forgives you. The Assassin does not forgive.

## What should an Assassin train first?

Combat: **Melee** for the close-range damage that pairs with backstabs and poisons — ranged casts make stealth approaches pointless. Money skills: **Fishing and Cooking**, because potions and consumables are your native language and the food chain feeds the same market (see [crafting overview](/crafting/crafting-overview/)). Full skill breakdown: [skills overview](/skills/skills-overview/).

## How does it compare?

Against the [Archer](/classes/archer/): double crit and stealth versus range and sustain. Against the [Ranger](/classes/ranger/): pure burst versus balanced durability. The side-by-side table: [which class to pick](/guides/which-class-to-pick/).
