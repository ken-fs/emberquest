The Archer is EmberQuest's pure ranged DPS: the game's fastest dash among the durable classes, regen perks, and a stat sheet built around never letting enemies close the gap.

## What is the Archer's identity?

"Pure ranged DPS with speed and regen perks" is the official one-liner, and the numbers back it. The Archer has the **second-fastest dash (1.86×)** in the game, self-sustain through regen talents, and the highest pierce value (7.6%) of any class — pierce being the stat that ignores enemy armor, which is exactly what you want when your damage arrives as many small hits against armored targets like other players' [Warrior](/classes/warrior/) friends.

## What are the Archer's base stats?

From the official wiki's live display (checked **October 11, 2026**, before gear; balance patches will move these):

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| HP | 11.0 |
| Armor | 0.1 |
| Resist | 4.1% |
| Heal bonus | 8.2% |
| Pierce | 7.6% |
| Crit chance | 6.0% |
| Crit damage | 52.0% |
| Backstab | 4.1% |
| Combat speed | 0.0% |
| Dash profile | 1.86× speed, 3.54× stamina |

The trade is clear: **11 HP and almost no armor** — one wrong pull against a boss is a death sentence — in exchange for range, pierce, and regen that keeps you topped up between fights.

## What does the dash profile mean in practice?

The Archer's dash is fast (1.86×) but burns 3.54× stamina, which changes how you fight: fewer, better dodges instead of constant repositioning. Pair that with the game's dash mechanics — dash is a core defensive tool for every class — and the Archer becomes a rhythm class. Kite until the enemy commits, dash through the commitment, punish, repeat.

## When should you pick Archer?

- You want ranged damage without the fragility trade-off being invisible — the Archer actually has sustain, unlike raw glass builds.
- You plan to fight armored targets (Warriors, world bosses with plates).
- You enjoy kiting gameplay and can respect a stamina bar.
- You mostly solo and want a class that clears field content from a safe distance.

Skip it if you want to frontload burst: the [Assassin](/classes/assassin/) doubles your crit chance and gets stealth on top. The Archer is a DPS — the Assassin is an executioner.

## What should an Archer train first?

Your combat skill is **Archery** — no debate, it is the class's native weapon line. For money, **Fishing** pairs beautifully with a ranged playstyle (low-risk, high-value food consumables), and **Chopping** feeds the wood armor line if you want a lightweight protective set without the metal grind. Details on every skill: [skills overview](/skills/skills-overview/); crafting basics: [crafting overview](/crafting/crafting-overview/).

## Archer vs Ranger vs Assassin — the one-line read

**Archer**: fastest class, safe damage, pierce for armor. **Ranger** ([full guide](/classes/ranger/)): more HP, a real armor value, slower dash — the ranged pick if 11 HP scares you. **Assassin** ([full guide](/classes/assassin/)): shorter range, double crit, stealth — the ranged pick if "safe" bores you.
